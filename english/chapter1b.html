CHAPTER 1B · 课文复习

The End of the Beginning

年轻小提琴家的音乐与意外

📚 分段理解与跟读

根据课本照片整理的分段学习版（不是逐字原文）。需要逐字阅读时请对照课本。

A young musician

Anne Jewinsky was a famous fifteen-year-old violinist. She started playing at five and won a youth competition at eight.

安妮·耶温斯基是一位15岁、已经成名的小提琴家。她五岁开始拉琴，八岁赢得青少年比赛。

The accident

One rainy and windy night in October, her father drove her home from a concert. They crashed into a stopped car, and Anne lost consciousness.

十月一个风雨交加的夜晚，父亲开车送她从音乐会回家。他们撞上了前方停着的车，安妮失去了意识。

In hospital

Anne woke up in hospital with two broken arms. The doctor said three fingers on her left hand might remain stiff, although she was lucky to be alive.

安妮在医院醒来，双臂骨折。医生说她左手的三根手指可能会僵硬，但她能活下来已是幸运。
