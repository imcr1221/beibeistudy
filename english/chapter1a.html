Down the Falls

尼亚加拉瀑布的惊险经历

📚 分段理解与跟读

根据课本照片整理的分段学习版（不是逐字原文）。需要逐字阅读时请对照课本。

A river trip

Roger Woodward was seven. His sister Deanne and Jim Honeycutt took a boat trip on the Niagara River. Deanne told Roger to put on his life jacket.

七岁的罗杰和姐姐迪安娜、吉姆一起乘船游览尼亚加拉河。姐姐提醒他穿上救生衣。

Danger ahead

They started about eight kilometres above the falls. Jim did not notice the point of no return. The engine stopped, and the boat filled with water.

他们从瀑布上游约八公里处出发。吉姆没有注意到已经过了无法返回的地点。发动机停了，船进了水。

What happened

Deanne swam to shore and was rescued. Roger and Jim fell over the falls. Jim died, but Roger survived because of his life jacket and was rescued by people on the Maid of the Mist.

迪安娜游向岸边获救。罗杰和吉姆跌下瀑布。吉姆遇难，但穿着救生衣的罗杰幸存，并被“雾中少女号”上的人救起。
