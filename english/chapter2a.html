Asking for explanations

A tourist in London does not understand “zebra crossing”. A Londoner explains that it means the white lines on the road.

伦敦的一位游客不理解“zebra crossing”（斑马线），当地人解释说就是路上的白色条纹。

Explaining unfamiliar words

A girl asks what a dog “lead” means. In another conversation, a man explains a parachute by describing how it is used.

一个女孩询问狗的“lead”（牵引绳）是什么意思。另一个对话里，有人通过用途解释降落伞。
